Use A/B/C only for a full candidate comparison: A is the robust baseline, B fixes its main demonstrated limitation, and C is a falsifiable innovation. Prefer the least complex chain whose validated performance, feasibility and interpretation satisfy the problem.

For an innovation portfolio, screen before selecting:

| Candidate/family | Baseline defect/evidence | Problem value | New information | Data support | Decisive test | Contest-budget risk | Interpretability | Paper clarity | Risk tier | Verdict/fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Only finalists earn a full innovation record and evidence level.
