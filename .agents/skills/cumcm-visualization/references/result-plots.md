# Result plots

## Prediction and fit

Use actual-vs-predicted with an identity line, residuals versus fitted, error distribution and forecast intervals. Never use the identity plot alone as validation.

## Optimization

Show the decision itself: route, schedule, allocation or Pareto front. Convergence plots describe solver behavior, not global optimality. Include feasibility or gap information when relevant.

## Evaluation and ranking

Show ranked scores with weight/rank uncertainty, winner probability or reversal ranges. Radar charts are rarely precise; use only for a small qualitative profile, not a ranking proof.

## Clustering

Use labeled projection or dendrogram only after defining distance/representation. A visually separated projection does not prove stable clusters.

## Sensitivity and robustness

Plot output against the perturbed parameter with baseline and decision thresholds. For stochastic experiments, show intervals, distributions or quantile bands. Caption the tested range and the stability conclusion.
