# Bidirectional problem-structure and model-family index

This is a routing index, not a topic-to-algorithm lookup table. Start from the formalized variables, governing relations, constraints, scale, objective, and uncertainty. Each historical case is evidence to inspect, not a prescribed answer.

## Problem structure -> model families

| Formalized structure | Candidate baseline | Add only when | Avoid / verify | Local evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Linked bodies on a parameterized curve with first-contact events | Geometry/kinematic recurrence plus a full collision predicate and bracketed event search | Scalar/nonconvex design variables remain after the mechanism is fixed | Do not replace governing geometry with GA/PSO; verify dimensions, boundary cases, event-time refinement, and full-body collision | [observed] Three 2024 A papers use spiral/link geometry before search. [ev-paper-2024-a-geometry-path, pp. 1-4; ev-paper-2024-a-dynamic-search-route, pp. 1, 13; ev-paper-2024-a-objective-optimization-form, pp. 1-3] |
| Small binary decision graph with inspection, disassembly, and feedback | Complete enumeration/decision tree with expected-profit ledger | State count makes enumeration impractical | Verify every transition and cost; a compressed state must retain all future-relevant history | [observed] 2024 B papers retain decision-tree, expected-profit, or state formulations. [ev-paper-2024-b-aco-ga-monte-carlo, pp. 3, 7-9; ev-paper-2024-b-multistage-simulation, pp. 1-2; ev-paper-2024-b-production-decision-design, pp. 1-2] |
| Multistage discrete process with Markov-sufficient state | Dynamic programming or exact state search | Exact state space is manageable or decomposable | Do not call a policy optimal until state sufficiency, recurrence, terminal conditions, and reduced-instance enumeration are checked | [observed] One 2024 B paper uses memoized dynamic programming after defining the state-decision process. [ev-paper-2024-b-production-decision-design, pp. 1-2] |
| Multi-period crop/land allocation with linear accounting and discrete operational rules | LP/MILP or transparent constrained program; greedy only as a feasible comparator | Nonlinear risk, scenario, or interaction terms are justified by the decision | Replay land/season/rotation/legume constraints and keep surplus accounting identical across methods | [observed] Four 2024 C papers formalize plot-year allocations before their search/uncertainty layers. [ev-paper-2024-c-optimization-planting, pp. 1-3; ev-paper-2024-c-differential-ga-planting, pp. 1-2; ev-paper-2024-c-ga-planting, pp. 1-3; ev-paper-2024-c-stochastic-planting, pp. 1-3] |
| Uncertain defect, yield, demand, cost, or price inputs | Nominal model plus scenario replay | Distribution/scenario assumptions are defensible and change a decision | Separate optimizer randomness from input uncertainty; report calibration, replications, intervals, and policy stability | [observed] Seven B/C cards add uncertainty after a nominal structure. [ev-paper-2024-b-multistage-simulation, p. 1; ev-paper-2024-b-aco-ga-monte-carlo, pp. 20-22; ev-paper-2024-b-production-decision-design, p. 1; ev-paper-2024-c-optimization-planting, pp. 18, 27; ev-paper-2024-c-differential-ga-planting, pp. 25-30, 42; ev-paper-2024-c-ga-planting, pp. 1-3; ev-paper-2024-c-stochastic-planting, pp. 1-3] |
| Small-sample nonlinear prediction, pure time series, classification, or ranking | Route to `cumcm-modeling` family prerequisites and a simple baseline | The current data structure supports the family | The ten-paper corpus supplies no direct case evidence; return no case rather than fabricate an analogy | [unverified] No supporting paper among the selected 2024 A/B/C set. |

## Model family -> problem structures

| Model family | Suitable structures and prerequisites | Not suitable / mandatory checks | Local cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism equations and geometric recurrences | Known geometry/physics, identifiable state, initial/boundary conditions, and interpretable governing relations | Not a substitute for empirical calibration when mechanisms/parameters are unknown; check units, conservation/closure, limits, and refinement | [observed] 2024 A linked spiral and turnaround models. [ev-paper-2024-a-geometry-path, pp. 1-4; ev-paper-2024-a-dynamic-search-route, p. 1; ev-paper-2024-a-objective-optimization-form, pp. 1-3] |
| Enumeration / decision tree | Small finite action space with auditable branch probabilities and payoffs | Avoid when unbounded feedback is not represented; verify branch completeness and accounting | [observed] Small-case 2024 B inspect/disassemble decisions. [ev-paper-2024-b-aco-ga-monte-carlo, p. 3] |
| Dynamic programming | Repeated subproblems and a Markov-sufficient state with explicit transitions and terminal value | Avoid if omitted history changes future reward/feasibility; compare reduced cases with enumeration | [observed] Memoized multistage production decisions. [ev-paper-2024-b-production-decision-design, pp. 1-2] |
| LP/MILP / deterministic constrained optimization | Linear objectives/constraints, continuous/integer allocations, and explicit feasibility | Do not use a metaheuristic merely for novelty; report status, violations, relaxation/bound/gap where available | [observed] Constrained 2024 C planting allocation; one paper explicitly compares a solver with greedy. [ev-paper-2024-c-stochastic-planting, pp. 1-3] |
| Monte Carlo / scenario analysis | Stochastic inputs with defensible distributions and outputs that need uncertainty propagation | Not evidence of real relationships by itself; require seeds, replications, uncertainty intervals, and scenario validity | [observed] Defect and crop-market scenario extensions. [ev-paper-2024-b-aco-ga-monte-carlo, pp. 20-22; ev-paper-2024-c-stochastic-planting, pp. 15-22] |
| Bayesian updating | Sequential defect/parameter evidence with an explicit likelihood and defensible prior | Avoid opaque priors; inspect prior sensitivity and posterior predictive behavior | [observed] Two 2024 B papers update defect-rate treatment. [ev-paper-2024-b-multistage-simulation, pp. 1-2; ev-paper-2024-b-production-decision-design, pp. 1-2] |
| CVaR / robust objective | Decision maker cares about tail loss or worst-case performance and uncertainty scenarios are calibrated | Uncertainty alone does not justify risk aversion; compare nominal objective, risk level, and feasibility under the same scenarios | [observed] 2024 C risk/robust extensions. [ev-paper-2024-c-differential-ga-planting, pp. 25-30, 42; ev-paper-2024-c-optimization-planting, pp. 18, 27] |
| GA / PSO / SA / ACO | Residual nonconvex or combinatorial search after the model and feasibility predicate are fixed, with exact methods unsuitable at scale | Never treat a solver name or one convergence trace as validity; require feasible encoding/repair, equal budgets, multiple seeds, dispersion, baseline, and bounds where possible | [observed] Search layers occur across A/B/C, but recurrence is coverage rather than validity evidence. [ev-paper-2024-a-dynamic-search-route, pp. 16-17, 28-29; ev-paper-2024-b-aco-ga-monte-carlo, pp. 7-9; ev-paper-2024-c-differential-ga-planting, pp. 1-2] |

For detailed prerequisites and implementation checks, continue to `../../cumcm-modeling/references/model-selection.md`, `model-families.md`, and the relevant validation reference. If this index and the current formalization disagree, the current problem structure wins.
